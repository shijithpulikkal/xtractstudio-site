Frequently Asked Questions

Do you build dashboards in Power BI or Looker Studio (Google Data Studio)?

Both. I build interactive dashboards in Power BI and Looker Studio depending on your team's tools and budget — Power BI for row-level security, deployment pipelines, and enterprise reporting; Looker Studio for lightweight, free-to-share dashboards connected to Google data sources.

Can you connect BigQuery, GA4, or retail media platforms to a dashboard?

Yes. I build BigQuery-based data pipelines that pull from GA4, Google Ads, Meta Ads, retail media APIs (Instacart Ads, Walmart Connect, CitrusAd), and other sources, then connect them to Power BI or Looker Studio for reporting.

Do you offer workflow automation, not just dashboards?

Yes. Alongside dashboards, I build automations with Python, Google Apps Script, Make.com, Zapier, n8n, and AI-assisted workflows to remove manual reporting work such as pulling ad platform exports or refreshing data feeds.
